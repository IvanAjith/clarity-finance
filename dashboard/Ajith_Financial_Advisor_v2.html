Allocation drift over time
Corpus: Glide Path vs Pure Equity
Why glide path? A 30% equity crash in your final year destroys ₹3+ Cr overnight on a pure-equity ₹10 Cr corpus. This optimised plan stays 100% equity for 20 years (maximum compounding), with Gold ETF at flat ₹1,000/month throughout for diversification. Bond Fund is paused Jan 2027 → redeemed ₹27,086 → added to equity. Bond resumes under the glide in 2047: 10% → 20% → 40% debt over the final 5 years. By retirement, 40% equity is in stable bond — a 30% crash only hits 60%, keeping corpus above ₹8.11 Cr even in the worst year. Total corpus: ₹9.85 Cr (equity ₹9.72 Cr + gold ₹13.6L). Safety cost: just ₹52.8L for that protection.
